04 · Data-driven design

Users who message on day one are 2× more likely to stay active

During the process I learned SQL and put together 60+ charts with the data analyst, plus 8 user interviews, to understand current response performance and find the factors that could improve response rate.

Sending a message on day one makes users roughly a third more likely to stay active — a clear, high-leverage moment to design toward.

05 · Ideation & prioritization

I facilitated ideation & prioritization sessions

From the data findings, I ran FigJam sessions with the team to brainstorm causes and solutions, then facilitated prioritization into a roadmap — including a dedicated session on how to encourage a first message.

06 · Design strategy

Built messaging into onboarding, and made chat visible everywhere

Previously, care seekers struggled to understand how to hire, and care providers struggled to understand how to apply. The new onboarding builds in message capability and user education; a message button and status now live right on the profile card and directory page.
