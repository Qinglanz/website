Designing a payment protection opt-in flow to boost revenue

A fully digital experience letting borrowers opt into Fig's Loan Payment Protection Plan during booking — balancing business objectives, legal compliance, and user needs.

ROLE

TEAM

STATUS
Launched
01 · The problem

Revenue beyond lending, protection beyond the loan

There was a business need for Fig to extend its revenue beyond lending, and a user need to protect their loan payment. Payment protection served both: additional revenue stream, reduced credit loss, and protection for borrowers.

02 · The challenge

Selling insurance without a salesperson

Insurance is typically a product that must be "sold" rather than "bought." The challenge: sell this complicated product without an agent, while minimizing user drop-off and ensuring legal compliance.

03 · The outcome

Transparency, simplicity, user control

A simple flow guided by three design principles: transparency, simplicity, and user control.

05 · Post-launch

Gathering feedback, aligning the roadmap

I conducted post-launch user interviews and reviewed session recordings to confirm the flow worked as expected, and partnered with product on the roadmap: pricing tests, product bundling, new entry points in the self-serve portal, and CX/marketing collaborations.
