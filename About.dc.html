How I work

At this level the job isn't producing screens — it's deciding which problems are worth solving, proving it with evidence I generate myself, and making the resulting change stick in the product and the process.

01 · FIND THE PROBLEM
Generate my own evidence

I write the SQL, read the support tickets, and run the interviews rather than waiting on a request queue — so problems surface before anyone assigns them.

02 · SIZE IT
Attach a number to the pain

Qualitative tells me why; funnel data tells me where and how much. A theme only becomes a priority when I can express it as revenue or conversion at a specific step.

03 · WIN THE BET
Pitch, don't request

I write the pitch — problem, evidence, appetite, solution, risks — and defend it at the betting table. Insights that stay in a report change nothing.

04 · DESIGN THE SYSTEM
Solve the class, not the case

Work within the design system and extend it where it's thin — so the fix holds across states, locales, partners, and the screens nobody demoed.

05 · DE-RISK & SHIP
Validate, then ship it myself

User testing and legal/risk review up front; feature flags and A/B plans on the way out. Low-risk UI changes I ship as draft PRs instead of tickets.

06 · MAKE IT COMPOUND
Leave the practice better

Track the metric after launch, feed what I learn back into the research pipeline, and turn one-off workflows into something the next designer can reuse.
